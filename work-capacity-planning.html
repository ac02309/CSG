Track record Enterprise digital commerce · Scheduling and capacity

A self-maintaining plan for 290+ client projects.

An operations team was planning a year of client projects manually. It now runs on an engine that schedules work against real capacity.

290+client projects in the plan
200+incoming projects scheduled
0unresolved conflicts

The problem

Projects ran across several regions, workstreams and delivery stages. The plan lived in a manually maintained spreadsheet, so every holiday, absence or late booking forced manual re-planning, and conflicts surfaced too late.

The system

A scheduling engine that allocates every project against available capacity, weighing:

  • the volume and complexity of each project
  • estimated effort per delivery stage
  • staff availability, holidays and absences
  • daily capacity per team

It flags projects at risk, recommends resourcing changes, re-plans within capacity, supports approvals for exceptions and records every change in an audit trail.

The result

More than 200 incoming projects were scheduled with zero unresolved conflicts. The team now manages by exception rather than rebuilding the plan.

  • Approvals
