How we work.

Six rules for every project.

  1. 1

    Automate the repeatable.

    If a task follows rules, a system should do it. Your team makes the judgement calls.

  2. 2

    Check every record.

    Every system checks its own work before anything goes live. Failures are held back, with the reason.

  3. 3

    Run unattended.

    Reports, logs and alerts are built in. You can see it working without checking by hand.

  4. 4

    Work worldwide.

    Based in the UK. We work worldwide, in your time zone.

  5. 5

    Deal direct.

    You work with the team that builds your system. One named lead, from the first call to go-live.

    No nonsense.
