How we work.

Six rules. Every project follows them.

  1. 01

    Automate the repeatable.

    If a task follows rules, a system should do it. Your team keeps the judgement calls, and the system handles the volume.

  2. 02

    Check every record.

    Every system we deliver validates its own output before anything goes live. Failures are held back with the reason recorded.

  3. 03

    Run unattended.

    Scheduled reporting, audit trails and monitoring are built in, so you can see a system working without checking it by hand.

  4. 04

    Work worldwide.

    UK-based, delivering worldwide. Sessions are scheduled to your time zone, and every enquiry is answered within one business day.

  5. 05

    Deal direct.

    Every project has a dedicated lead engineer who scopes, builds and supports it: a single point of accountability from discovery to go-live.

  6. 06

    No nonsense.

    Published prices, a fixed written scope and plain English. Nothing is committed until you approve the plan.

Four stages, brief to live system.

Your involvement: a short written brief, then one to two hours a week from a process owner.

  1. 01 / 04

    Discovery brief

    You set out the process, the volumes and the systems involved. The engineer who will lead your project reviews it.

    A short written brief
  2. 02 / 04

    Solution demo

    A session built around your process: what we can automate, how it works, and the plan and investment to deliver it.

    20 minutes
  3. 03 / 04

    Build and integration

    Engineered into your existing systems, validated against your live data and delivered to an agreed scope.

    Most builds: 2 to 6 weeks
  4. 04 / 04

    Go live and support

    Deployed, documented and handed over, with ongoing support as your operations evolve.

    30 days of defect cover
