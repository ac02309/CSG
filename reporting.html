What changes.

The manual work it removes, and what you have instead.

Today

  • Hours spent compiling the same reports each week
  • Copying figures between spreadsheets
  • Figures that cannot be relied on

With reporting

  • Scheduled email reporting
  • Live dashboards in Power BI, Excel or Google Sheets

  • Alerts when metrics move outside tolerance

How it works.

Four steps, built around your process and your systems.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Data gathered automatically from every system the report depends on.

  2. 2

    Validate

    Figures checked against your rules before anyone sees them.

  3. 3

    Assemble

    The report or dashboard built the same way every time.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    Sent on schedule to the people who need it, with alerts when a metric moves outside tolerance.
