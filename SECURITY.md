# Reporting a vulnerability

Email **enquiries@clarkesystemsgroup.com** with "Security" in the subject. Include the page or file, what you found and how to reproduce it.

- We reply within two business days.
- We fix confirmed issues in the live site within seven days, or tell you why and when.
- Please don't access data that isn't yours, run denial-of-service tests or use automated scanners at volume.
- We won't take action against good-faith research that follows this policy. We credit you if you want us to.

The same contact is published at `/.well-known/security.txt`.
