FAQ

Questions, answered.

Cost, timing, data and ownership. Plain answers.

Cost and timing

How much does it cost?

The demo and written plan are free. Builds start at £1,250 (US$1,500). Support starts at £200 (US$250) a month. No VAT is added. Larger projects start with a paid scoping session, £400 (US$500), taken off the build price.

How long does it take?

Most builds take two to six weeks. Your written plan gives the exact timeline.

How much of our time will it take?

A short written brief. Then one to two hours a week from the person who runs the process.

Systems, data and ownership

Will it work with the systems we already use?

Yes. We work with the systems you have. Nothing is replaced. We confirm every link before we build.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We only access what the project needs, and we sign your NDA first. How we keep data safe.

Do we own what is built?

Yes. On final payment, the code, documents and accounts are yours. Any shared parts come with a lasting licence.

Working together

What if something breaks, or our process changes?

We fix any fault in the agreed scope for free for 30 days after handover. After that, support covers changes.

Who is behind Clarke Systems Group?

A team of engineers and specialists who build automation systems. The case studies show systems built and run at a global retail-data business: launch checks across 30+ markets, planning for 290+ client projects, and AI product sorting.

Who will we work with?

Our team: engineers who build and test your system, and specialists in data, AI and delivery. One named lead runs your project from start to finish.

Where are you based, and when can we talk?

In the UK, working in your time zone.
