Catalogue checks for online stores

We check every product in your catalogue for the data errors that break collections, shipping rates, stock sync and shopping feeds. You get the product-level list and a fixed price to fix it.

What we check

  • SKUs. Missing SKUs, and SKUs shared by more than one variant. Both break stock and order matching with warehouses and marketplaces.
  • Categories. Products with no category, and near-duplicate category names that split one range across two places.
  • Shipping data. Shipped variants with no weight, so weight-based rates, labels and marketplace postage are calculated on zero.
  • Content. Products with no image, thin descriptions, duplicate titles and titles too long for Google Shopping.
  • Pricing. Stale sale prices and products priced at zero.
  • Brand names. One brand spelt several ways, which splits brand filters and reporting.

How it works

  1. Send your store address. Nothing else is needed to start.
  2. We run the checks. Read-only, on your public catalogue. We never ask for admin access at this stage.
  3. You receive the report. Every flagged product, why it matters, and a sample of our AI categorisation run against your own categories. Within two business days.
  4. We fix it at a fixed price. Agreed before any work starts. Nightly checks then catch new issues before customers or marketplaces do.

Why us

Our catalogue checks were proven on a 30+ market product launch at a global digital-commerce analytics business, where one wrong record repeats across every retailer and market. We apply the same discipline to your store.
