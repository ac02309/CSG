    Track record Scheduling and capacity · built in-house, before Clarke Systems Group

    A plan for 290+ client projects that keeps itself up to date.

    An operations team was planning a year of client projects by hand. It now runs on a system that schedules work against real capacity.

    290+client projects in the plan
    200+incoming projects scheduled
    0unresolved clashes

    The problem

    Projects ran across several regions, workstreams and delivery stages. The plan lived in a manually maintained spreadsheet, so every holiday, absence or late booking meant re-planning by hand, and clashes were found late.

    The system

    A scheduling engine that places every project against the capacity the team actually has. It weighs:

    • the volume and complexity of each project
    • estimated effort per delivery stage
    • staff availability, holidays and absences
    • daily capacity per team

    It flags projects at risk, recommends resourcing changes, re-plans within capacity, supports approvals for exceptions and records every change in an audit trail.

    The result

    More than 200 incoming projects were scheduled with no unresolved clashes. The team now works on the exceptions the system flags, instead of rebuilding the plan.

    Track record Quality checks and reporting · built in-house, before Clarke Systems Group

    A 30+ market launch where errors were caught before release.

    A global product launch had to go live across dozens of retailers on daily deadlines. Every record was tracked and checked automatically.

    4,000+records checked
    180+errors caught before release
    Dailyprogress reports, compiled automatically

    The problem

    The launch covered more than 30 markets and over 100 retailer lines, with a team working to daily deadlines. Every record had to be right before release, and manual checking couldn't keep pace.

    The system

    A tracking and quality-control system that:

    • logged every record the team produced each day
    • ran automated checks against the launch rules
    • held back anything that failed, with the reason attached
    • sent daily progress reports without anyone compiling them

    It was built alongside running delivery for the launch, so every check was shaped by the deadline it had to meet.

    The result

    More than 180 errors were caught automatically before release, and daily progress reports went out without anyone compiling them.

    Track record AI processing and integrations · built in-house, before Clarke Systems Group

    Manual categorisation, replaced by a measured AI service.

    Products were being categorised by hand. A language-model service now does it, with the model chosen on tested accuracy and cost.

    3AI model families tested
    AWShosted and access-controlled
    MFAplus full audit logging

    The problem

    Categorising products by hand was slow and inconsistent, and the data involved needed proper access control.

    The system

    A classification service hosted on AWS. Before a model was chosen, OpenAI, Anthropic and self-hosted open-weight models were benchmarked on accuracy against real categories and on cost per classification.

    • model chosen on measured accuracy and cost
    • sign-in with multi-factor authentication and audit logging
    • a database designed for the results
    • connected to the team's ticketing and reporting tools
    • a dashboard tracking the model's accuracy over time

    The result

    A manual workflow replaced by a service with known accuracy and a known cost per item, connected to the team's existing tools and protected by multi-factor sign-in and audit logging.

    FAQ.

    Straight answers on cost, timing, data and ownership.

    How much does it cost?

    The assessment is free. Builds start from £1,250 (US$1,500) and support from £200 (US$250) a month, excluding VAT. Every project is priced on its scope, and your assessment includes a written outline and estimate, so you can decide before anything is agreed. For larger projects, a paid scoping session, £400 (US$500), is credited in full against the build.

    How long does it take?

    Typical builds take two to six weeks, depending on scope. Your estimate includes the timeline.

    Will it work with the systems we already use?

    Usually. Projects connect to the tools you already run, so there's nothing to replace, and every connection is confirmed during the assessment.

    How much of our time will it take?

    Very little. A short written description to start, then someone who knows the process to answer questions and test the result: about an hour or two a week.

    Is our data safe?

    Access is limited to what the project needs, credentials stay in your accounts, and data is only processed by services you approve. AI work runs on business API terms, under which your data is not used to train models by default. We're happy to sign your NDA before you share any details.

    What if something breaks or our process changes?

    Fixes for anything that doesn't work as agreed are included for 30 days after handover. For changes as your process evolves, monthly support is available.

    Do we own what's built?

    Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the code, documentation and accounts are yours. Any reusable components brought to the project are licensed to you permanently.

    Who will we be working with?

    The engineer who scopes your system is the one who builds it, and stays your point of contact through handover and support. Nothing is passed down a chain.

    Where are you based, and when can we talk?

    Clarke Systems Group is UK-based and available to teams in the UK, US, Europe and Australia. Work runs asynchronously, calls are by arrangement in UK or US hours, and messages are answered within one business day.
