How we work.

Six rules. Every project follows them.

  • Automate the repeatable.

    If a task follows rules, a system should do it. Your team keeps the judgement calls.

  • Check every record.

    Every system validates its own output before anything goes live, and records why anything failed.

  • Run unattended.

    Scheduled reporting, audit trails and accuracy monitoring are built in, so you can see a system working without checking it by hand.

  • Work worldwide.

    UK-based, delivering worldwide. Sessions are scheduled to your time zone.

  • Deal direct.

    The engineer who scopes your system builds and supports it. One accountable lead, from first call to go-live.

  • No nonsense.

    Published prices, a fixed written scope and plain English. Nothing is committed until you approve the plan.
